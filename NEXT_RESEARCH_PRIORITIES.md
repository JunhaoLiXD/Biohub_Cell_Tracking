## Prioritized experiment proposals

### 1. Multi-frame tracklet association

**Hypothesis.** Tracklet history, future consistency, and appearance can resolve discontinuities that the current two-frame edge model and local motion repairs leave fragmented, without losing division precision.

**Exact first change.** Starting from repro_041 detections and features, replace one explicitly delimited ambiguous-link/gap-decision module with a tracklet action scorer using a preregistered short temporal window, for example five frames. Encode displacement in physical units, appearance consistency, uncertainty, and competing tracklets. Retain confident parent links. Use an unchanged deterministic conflict resolver for the first scoring ablation; test a new joint decoder separately. The initial existing-node pilot must not also create synthetic nodes or retune detector/EMA settings.

Train on actual parent proposals, with hard competing candidates and sparse-label-aware supervision. Labels with genuinely annotated incompatible identities may serve as negatives; unannotated cells must remain unknown. Fit a compact temporal attention or message-passing head first, then consider joint representation training if frozen features cannot separate actions. The scientific novelty is tracklet context and deployment-matched learning, not model size.

**Go/stop.** Proceed when the current candidate-constrained audit exposes a meaningful positive joint repair opportunity in both specimens. Require out-of-sample action discrimination and actual final-graph improvement; AUROC alone is insufficient. Stop or redirect to detection if missing endpoints dominate the attainable repairs. Stop this model recipe if it increases edge FP or causes dense-field collapse despite improving training loss.

**Expected value and risk.** This has the broadest directly observed error pool and can borrow supervision from continuation edges, but longer windows can propagate a wrong link. Explicit birth/death options, abstention, division protection, and bounded local neighborhoods must prevent forced associations. Temporal windows must respect clip boundaries. The full architecture may require more compute than the present pilot budget; that does not lower its research priority.

### 2. Image-informed division and division-preserving decoding

**Hypothesis.** A representation shared with abundant continuation examples can recognize mother-to-two-daughter events more reliably than scalar center confidence or graph-only endpoint ranking, while a lineage-aware decoder preserves the useful predictions.

**Two causally distinct pilots.** If the audit finds good division candidates removed by one-to-one motion relinking, first test preserving a fixed, independently supported subset through that stage, with scorer-exact FP protection. If candidates survive but are scored poorly, train an image/temporal division action head on parent/daughter crops and existing embeddings; change the scorer while initially freezing candidate generation and conflict resolution. Do not combine a new head, relaxed radii, and altered caps in one unexplained experiment.

A later joint model would predict continuation, division, and no-link actions from a common representation, with at most one incoming parent and at most two outgoing daughters, and decode them jointly. A good upstream fork must not be silently replaced by a later one-to-one pass. Existing post-link division logic should be replaced at a documented interface, not stacked indiscriminately on top of new splits.

**Data and go/stop.** Train16 contains only 12 scored GT divisions, not enough to fit this model. Historical data inspection reports 151 total events, 125 in 6bba and 26 in 44b6; re-audit usable independent events and annotations before training. Use grouped splits, shared continuation learning, appropriate imbalance handling, and reliable negatives in annotated regions. Measure precision-recall at the actual deployment candidate prevalence and final joint graph edits. Require improvement on both specimen directions and stable FP behavior on division-negative videos. Stop if apparent success is just fitting one or two known events or identifying annotation coverage.

This remains a high-priority architecture direction despite failed tabular Family-A policies. It adds image/time information and changes how splits survive, addressing two limitations those policies did not resolve.

### 3. Targeted temporal detection and missing-daughter recovery

**Hypothesis.** A context-conditioned, higher-resolution recovery head can restore real missing cells or daughters where the present detector and filtering lose them, improving lineage recovery without globally inflating density.

**Exact first change.** Choose one failure class from the audit: proposal miss, localization failure, or downstream deletion. For proposal misses, evaluate a small residual detector/refiner around label-free uncertain track endpoints and potential daughter neighborhoods, using adjacent-frame images and physical-scale coordinates. Compare against the current two-frame temporal detector; do not describe it as a static detector. Existing DeepCenter-confirmed gap insertion is already present, so merely adding another center threshold is not this proposal. If filtering caused the loss, test the relevant filter decision before retraining detection.

**Go/stop.** Require additional matched endpoints and recovered official edges/divisions, with acceptable overall density and no material precision regression. A heatmap-recall gain or more emitted nodes is insufficient. Freeze nomination criteria before validation; ground-truth missing nodes cannot nominate candidates during inference. Stop if the candidate only recovers unscored nodes, if density costs erase edge gains, or if high-resolution information does not separate missed cells from ambiguity.

The old high-recall detector loss (0.827 to 0.786 with the contemporary linker), wider-model neutrality, and augmentation loss are relevant warnings. They do not rule out targeted temporal recovery under the current model, but strongly oppose a global threshold reduction or a width-only retraining campaign. Source: [selected historical experiments](docs/experiments.md).

### 4. Confidence-adaptive association fusion

**Hypothesis.** Primary/secondary confidence disagreement contains useful reliability information that current low-margin consensus does not exploit on the retained parent.

The [archived public comparison](.private/research/public_0941_comparison_2026-09-05.md) identifies Rishabh's adaptive arbitration as a distinct mode using calibrated logits and model margins. ### 5. Joint detection, embedding, and lineage architecture

**Hypothesis.** If candidate pruning and independent detector/linker decisions discard mutually supporting explanations, jointly learning identity features and selecting node/edge/split hypotheses can recover events unavailable to frozen-node repairs.

Three concrete alternatives deserve technical investigation, with one selected after the audit:

| Architecture route | Difference from the current pipeline | Main compatibility question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CELLECT-inspired contrastive identity and division heads | Explicit identity-oriented representation and division supervision, rather than a generic replacement U-Net | Can sparse annotations provide valid contrastive pairs/negatives, and can its outputs match the competition's point graph and anisotropy? |
| Ultrack-inspired multiple detection hypotheses with joint lineage optimization | Preserve competing node hypotheses until temporal evidence selects them | Are credible foreground/contour or equivalent point hypotheses available, and can the offline solver handle dense volumes within limits? |
| Shared-trunk temporal point model with continuation/division/birth heads | Train predictions on real proposals and preserve their semantics through decoding | Can training and inference use the same proposal distribution and produce a viable out-of-sample gain? |

These are architectural inspirations, not proven compatible replacements or promised pretrained gains. First compare a narrow module against a matched control. If full retraining is needed, establish a separate grouped validation protocol and adequate budget. A new architecture that uses the same already-seen backbone is not an independent generalization test.

### 6. Complementary-model ensemble

**Hypothesis.** A candidate from priorities 1–5 makes sufficiently different correct decisions to improve the parent through calibrated selection.

Measure where each model helps and harms, then an offline oracle-selection ceiling using the same scorer. If the ceiling is small, stop. If substantial, learn a deployment-available reliability rule on training folds and assess the actual ensemble on untouched folds. Align node identities/coordinates, resolve edge and division conflicts, and track inference cost. Unioning graphs or averaging association scores from differently calibrated models is not a sufficient design. Generic third-seed training, TTA, and another similar public notebook rank low without complementary-error evidence.

## Validation, decision gates, and budget

Keep `public_0941_frozen_train16_stratified_proxy_v1` unchanged for inference comparisons. Preserve sample selection/order, scorer functions, density metadata, checkpoint loading identity, and per-video/per-specimen reporting. The metric is `adjusted_edge_jaccard + 0.1 * division_jaccard`; it is not a conventional normalized weighted average. Neither run determinism nor repeated use of the same 16 videos establishes generalization.

The frozen per-video adjustment is `max(0, J * (1 - 0.1 * (T_pred - T_true) / T_true))` when the estimated true node count is positive; otherwise it returns raw `J`. The signed deviation can make adjusted Jaccard exceed raw Jaccard. Report raw edge counts/Jaccard and node-count effects separately. A density-only gain with unchanged edge and division confusion counts does not validate a claimed association/division improvement. Genuine FP reduction or division recovery can still be useful without increasing edge TP; do not impose an indiscriminate TP-only objective.

For learned additions, separate training, model selection, and evaluation by original acquisition/lineage groups and disjoint temporal context, not random crops or adjacent frames. Report leave-one-specimen-out stress tests as well as grouped within-specimen validation where feasible. A head trained on one specimen with a backbone that saw both is still a leaky-backbone test. Such a head-only comparison is useful as explicitly labeled screening if fitting excludes evaluation labels; it is not independent generalization evidence. ## External architecture references

Primary sources checked on 2026-09-08 support the existence and mechanism of these approaches, not competition-specific gains:

- [Trackastra paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15700) and [authors' repository](https://github.com/weigertlab/trackastra): transformer associations over temporal windows with division support. Its API consumes images and instance masks. Our old failed trials and pseudo-mask/domain issues take precedence over generic benchmark claims; the SAM2 feature variant documented there is explicitly a 2D model.
- [CELLECT authors' repository](https://github.com/zzz333za/CELLECT): two-frame U-Net features/centers, contrastive identity representation, and within/across-frame MLPs including division decisions. Reusing its learning objective is a distinct option from inserting another two-frame U-Net. Sparse-label conversion, offline packaging, checkpoints, and license terms require inspection before adoption.
- [Ultrack authors' repository](https://github.com/royerlab/ultrack): multiple segmentation hypotheses and temporal consistency in 2D/3D crowded tissue. Its foreground/contour interface is not directly interchangeable with the present centroid graph.
- [OrganoidTracker authors' repository](https://github.com/jvzonlab/OrganoidTracker): learned link/division probabilities and contextual error confidence. This supports investigating calibrated action reliability in priorities 1–2, not assuming organoid-trained weights transfer to this dataset.
- [EmbedTrack paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.10713): joint segmentation/tracking through learned offsets, evaluated on 2D datasets. It is a lower-fit reference for this anisotropic 3D point-annotation task, so it is not recommended as the first replacement.
